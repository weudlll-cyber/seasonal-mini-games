## Owner questions

The first draft's questions (a)–(p) were answered on 2026-10-24 (DECISIONS O8–O15, T21). Questions (q), (s), (t) and (u) and most of (w) were answered on 2026-11-01 (O29–O33). Each one below is built as written unless the owner decides otherwise; (r) waits for the reference VPS.

**(r) Load gate at 1 000 players.** One plain shared round each, on the development machine (Windows laptop, server under `tsx` in development mode, driver on the same machine), not the reference VPS of 09 §5. 500 players + 100 spectators: acknowledgement p95 133 ms / p99 190 ms, feedback p95 133 ms, nothing lost, duplicated or reordered, one winner, memory 357 MiB; event-loop lag p99 peaked at 87 ms (limit 50 ms). 1 000 players: the fairness checks all hold, but acknowledgement p95 is 4.6 s (limit 300 ms): the server's one thread is saturated, and the profile shows socket writes as the main cost (DECISIONS T49 already merges room frames). Proposal: measure L1 and L4 on the reference VPS before changing the design; if L4 still fails there, raise `SMG_ROOM_FRAME_MS` for large rounds or move the socket fan-out off the round's thread (04 §5.6). Numbers: `reports/night-1/load-500.json`, `load-1000.json`. Night 8 (DECISIONS T180–T184): the driver plays L1's rounds, each alone or all in one match (ten rounds since T200: five plain, the four specials, the halving round) (`node load/guessStorm.mjs <players> [out.json] <scenario>`), and each ran once with 200 players + 100 spectators on the development machine (laptop busy with other agents' work, not a gate): every round kept the fairness checks (proof, gap-free, nothing lost, duplicated or reordered, one winner), and the specials fired on time on the server (Spring Herald 8 ms, Heatwave ≤ 4 ms, Leaf Fall p95 30 ms, halving 11 ms after their due time). Acknowledgement p95: plain 731 ms, spring 163 ms, summer 4.3 s, autumn 317 ms, halving 358 ms, winter 5.6 s. The cause on this machine is the round's serial commit chain: a batch took 41–46 ms to commit (plain: 1 200 batches in a 66 s round), and under Frost every release of held answers is a batch of its own (`HeldDelivered`), so the winter round had 2 009 batches in 106 s; the server released the held answers on time (all 4 202 within 100 ms), but they and the acknowledgements waited behind earlier commits, so Frost answers arrived p95 5.3 s after their due time (limit 100 ms). Proposal: measure on the reference VPS first; if Frost still misses there, let a release ride in the next guess batch instead of a commit of its own, or send released answers without waiting for the `HeldDelivered` commit (04 §5.3). Numbers: `reports/night-8/load-l1-*-200.json`.

**(w) The chat on screen (builtin).** 07 §2 names the providers but not where the chat sits. Built as proposed: in the round view the match chat in the right column under the team tiles (its list scrolls inside itself, the round view stays one screen), on a phone behind a "Chat" button that opens it over the round with the guess input still in view; before and between rounds the match chat as a panel of its own under the waiting area (on a phone behind its "Chat" button, DECISIONS T116), and the lobby chat at the foot of the lobby, each in a box whose list scrolls inside itself (DECISIONS T101); "Report" on every line, and for moderators Delete, Mute 5 min / 1 h / 24 h and Ban, each with a reason and a confirm sentence; an inline mute or ban applies to the whole chat (a ban from the admin API may be kept to one room). Owner decision 32 (2026-11-01) answered two of its questions: a mute covers the whole chat, and the round-end line names the number (T90). Still built as proposed: staff see no mark on flagged messages in the room; flagged messages go to the moderation queue.

**(x) Staff actions as players see them.** Built as proposed: a staff abort reads "The round was aborted: by staff (reason)"; a kicked player who tries to join again is refused with KICKED, "You were removed from this match by the host." (night run 3, the wording given in the task, although staff do the kicking); a removed result still appears in that round's result view, only the standings and boards leave it out (T93).

**(y) The game embedded.** Built as proposed: inside the host's frame the view shows no top bar, only a small "Signed in as <name>" line; a match opened from the embedded lobby opens inside the frame; a page that is not allowlisted, if it ever loads the frame, shows "This site may not show Seasonal Mini Games." The former open points are built (night run 3): an account made by the handoff deletes itself with its display name typed (DECISIONS T105), on the account page's "Delete my account" (T170, confirmed by the owner 2026-11-04); in a standalone install `antiMulti.requireLinkedChat` cannot be switched on, the admin form says why, and the server refuses it (T103); `antiMulti.minAccountAgeH` is applied to shared rosters (T103).

**(z) Closeness comments (owner decisions 20 and 21, 2026-10-31).** Built as proposals and adjustable in the admin app: the tier thresholds in percent of the range's width — way off above 50 %, cold above 25 %, cool above 10 %, warm above 3 %, hot above 1 %, burning at or under 1 % (`comments.*Above`); and how often a change of tier gets a trend line instead of a tier line, 33 % (`comments.trendPercent`) (02 §6.3, 08 §3). Lettered (z) because the night branch uses (t)–(y).

**(aa) The fairness proof and player numbers (2026-11-01).** Public answers now name players by their number in the match, never by account id (DECISIONS T104, closing the former question (v)). In an individual round each player's secret is derived from the seed with the label `secret/<account id>`, so an outsider holding only the published proof can check the commitment and the shared secret, but cannot recompute an individual player's secret without that player's account id; the server's own check (`/rounds/:id/verify`) recomputes and shows them all. Proposal: keep it so; a new seed version could derive individual secrets from the match number instead, which would change nothing for rounds already played (their stored seeds keep the old labels).

**(ab) The countdown before a round (2026-11-01).** The countdown is now the round's intro screen (owner decision 36): the season, its special and what it does, the secret and its range, the cooldown and the seconds left. Proposal: `round.countdownS` 10 s instead of 5 s, so the screen can be read; the range stays 3–30 s (02 §3, 08 §3; DECISIONS T109).

**(af) Seasonal Mind Games (2026-11-12).** The owner decided the game (O76–O90); everything below was not given and is written into spec/02e, 04 §4.8, 05, 08 §3.4 and 09 as a proposal, built as written unless the owner decides otherwise:

1. Season specials: none in this game (each of Spring Herald, Heatwave, Leaf Fall and Frost would need its own design). Should it have any?
2. Halving rounds: none in this game. Should a halving day change anything?
3. Season teams: none in this game. Should a team score when its members win?
4. Ties in the winner rule: places are dense (counts 3, 3, 5, 9 give places 1, 1, 2, 3, so "2nd least chosen" is the 5), rather than skipping (1, 1, 3, 4, where no 2nd place exists).
5. Bounds: total 30–600 s, delay 5–120 s and at most (total − 10 s) / 2, join time 10–590 s and at most total − delay, rounds 1–20, pause 5–120 s.
6. The join time in the host form: a box "Join time follows the total" (on) beside the number field, which applies only with the box off.
7. The number of rounds and the pause are chosen in the first round's form and hold for the whole match.
8. During a pause the host's next-round form is open; what is saved before the next round starts applies to it.
9. After the last round's pause the match ends by itself with "All 6 rounds are played." and the summary.
10. Later rounds of a series start without the minimum-players check; a round nobody enters ends "Nobody chose this round."
11. A click on a choice button sends the choice at once; there is no separate Send.
12. The order and words of the refusals (02e §5.2): "Join the match to play.", "Choices are closed for this round.", "Joining this round has closed. You can play the next round.", "All is not offered in this round.", "That is already your choice."
13. The feed's words: "The first choices show in 12 s.", "The round 30 s ago: 23 players", "All 23 players are in. Every change now is a switch.", lines with their age counting on ("· 31 s ago"); the view keeps the newest 60 lines.
14. A first choice made by a pre-pick looks like any other in the feed (no "away" mark).
15. "Hide my switches" stays as set for the rest of the match, kept by the server with the pre-picks.
16. Pre-picks are set in the waiting area (before the first round and in each pause), not during a round; a round's pre-pick locks when it starts.
17. A pre-pick of All for a round without All gives no choice, and the player's own view says so; leaving the match drops all pre-picks.
18. The host's End round fixes the final choices at that moment; Abort round as in every game.
19. The result shows final choices, counts, places and points; switch histories and pre-picks are never shown, not even after the end. Its headlines and the chat line ("Round 3: Winter was the least chosen (3 players).").
20. The round keeps its season (In Season or the host's pick) for its colours and intro, with "No special this round."; it changes no rule.
21. No fairness proof: nothing in a round is random, so no commitment and no verification page; the result itself shows everything needed to check it.
22. The screen: a phase strip with "now" and "feed" markers, choice buttons with the delayed count under each, the lines on the right; on a desktop it stays whole with the Chat panel open; a later call view gets about 360 × 203 px above the chat on a desktop and a sheet of its own on a phone.
23. The intro line: "Pick a season. The least chosen wins." with the timing in one line.
24. Pre-picks travel by `match.prefs`, at most once every 2 s per account.
25. The Quick Test: 7 bots by default; about one in four pre-picks and stays away; the others enter spread over the join time and stay, switch at random, or lean to the least chosen option in the feed they see.
26. A load scenario L5: one round with 500 players switching up to every 2 s.
27. Animation (O90): flights run in a band between the choice buttons and their counts, from count to count, clipped to the stage, never over a button, a feed line or the chat.
28. Animation: a first choice's icon flies from the player's name line into the season, with the name on it.
29. Animation: a hidden switch (switches hidden, or "Hide my switches" ticked) still flies from the old season to the new one, without a name.
30. Animation: all switches of one feed step fly as one wave, at most 12 icons at once with a "+8" tag for the rest, about 0.8 s a flight; the counts are always the step's exact counts.
31. Animation: at the round end the winning season or seasons light up for about 2 s on the result, beside their check and "wins".
32. Animation: with the device's reduced-motion setting there are no flights and no light-up; the counts change in place.

## Needed from the website

Found in the dress rehearsal of 2026-11-06 (website `master` at `82e9e30`, which holds `feat/embedded-games` `468aaca`; Mini Games `feat/website-mode`; nginx mode on a fresh Ubuntu 24.04 machine).

1. **The login does not come back to the game.**
   - Step: a guest in the game clicks "Sign in" (or the chat's "Log in"), logs in on the website's login page.
   - Request: `GET /games/mini-games/auth/website?next=%2Fgames%2Fmini-games%2F` → 302 to `/login?next=%2Fgames%2Fmini-games%2Fauth%2Fwebsite%3Fnext%3D%252Fgames%252Fmini-games%252F`; then the login form (`POST /api/auth/login`, 200).
   - Answer: the website's own app moves to `/games/mini-games/auth/website?next=%2Fgames%2Fmini-games%2F` inside the page (`navigate(safeNext(next))` in `client/src/pages/LoginPage.jsx` `finish`) and shows its "Page not found"; no request reaches the game's sign-in route, so the member is not signed in to the game. A reload of that address signs in at once.
   - Expected: a full page load of `next` when it is not one of the website's own app routes (for example `window.location.assign(next)` for paths under `/games/`), so the proxy's sign-in route runs and the member lands back on the game page. The same `navigate(safeNext(next))` is in `RegisterPage.jsx` and `DiscordSignupPage.jsx`; the Discord login's server-side redirect (`server/src/discord/routes.js`) is not affected. The same holds for Bingo at `/games/bingo/`.
